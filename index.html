Who We Are

About Scenic Travel Agency

We're a locally owned travel management company with a simple belief: travel should be seamless, personal, and stress-free.

Built in PNG, for PNG

Scenic Travel Agency was founded right here in Port Moresby because we saw a gap — a need for a travel partner that truly understands the unique rhythm of doing business and travelling in Papua New Guinea. From the highlands to the islands, we know the routes, the challenges, and the opportunities.

We work with government agencies, corporate clients, NGOs, and families, tailoring every trip to fit real needs — not a one-size-fits-all template. Our team brings decades of combined experience, but more importantly, we bring a genuine care for the people we serve.

Your Journey, Our Commitment

We don't just book flights — we build trust, one trip at a time. Our local knowledge and global reach mean you're in good hands wherever you're headed.

When we started STA, we wanted to build something different. A travel agency that doesn't just process bookings, but truly partners with its clients. Travel in Papua New Guinea comes with its own flavour — the remote airstrips, the weather that changes without warning, the need for someone who knows the lay of the land. We've lived it, and we've built our business around it.

Our team genuinely enjoys helping people get where they need to go, whether that's a government delegation heading to a conference, a family planning their first overseas holiday, or a business executive who needs to be in three different provinces in a single week. We take pride in getting the details right so our clients can focus on their work and their lives.

I invite you to reach out, ask questions, and give us the chance to show you what travel care really looks like.

Managing Director, Scenic Travel Agency
